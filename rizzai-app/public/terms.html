Terms & Privacy

RizzByJulius · last updated 3 October 2026

1. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. By creating an account you confirm you are.

2. How to use it

  • The replies are suggestions. You are responsible for what you send.
  • Respect other people. If someone says no or stops replying, back off.
  • No harassment, pressure, threats, deception, impersonation, explicit sexual content or anything illegal.
  • We can remove accounts that break these rules.

3. Your data

We keep your phone number, username, a scrambled (hashed) password, how many times you used the app, and your payment records. Chat screenshots and text you submit are sent to an AI service to write replies. Screenshots are not stored by us. Your history of suggestions stays on your own device. We do not sell your data.

4. Free tries and payments

New accounts get a number of free tries. After that you pay the price shown in the app for a set number of days of access. Payments are for access time and are generally not refundable, but contact us if something went wrong.

5. No guarantees

We can't promise any result with anyone you message. The service may change or be unavailable at times.

6. Contact

Use the WhatsApp link on the home page for help or questions.
